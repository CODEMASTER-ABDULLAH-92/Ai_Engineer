# **Additional Tips & Resources**

1. **Time Management**
- Spend at least 2–3 hours daily on weekdays, and 4–5 hours on weekends if possible.
- Consistency is key. If you skip days, ensure you catch up on critical topics.
1. **Coding Platforms**
- **GeeksforGeeks**: Great for theoretical + basic to advanced DSA practice.
- **LeetCode**: Focused on interview-style problems.
- **HackerRank** / **CodeChef**: Good for competitive programming & timed practice.
1. **Focus on Problem-Solving Approach**
- Before coding, always think about constraints, possible edge cases, and optimal approach.
- Write pseudocode or outline your approach to avoid confusion.
1. **Peer Learning / Community**
- Join study groups or forums to discuss solutions, get hints, and stay motivated.
- If possible, do pair programming or mock interviews with peers.
1. **Mentor or Guide**
- If you can, find a mentor who can do code reviews for you.
- Regular feedback accelerates learning.
